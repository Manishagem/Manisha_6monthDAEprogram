# Risk Management

This folder contains documents related to risk management within the EISP (Enterprise Identity Security Platform). Key documents include:
- `01-EISP-risk-management-fundamentals.docx`: Covers fundamental concepts of risk management.
- `02-EISP-risk-assessment-techniques.docx`: Details various techniques for risk assessment.
- `03-EISP-risk-evaluation-methods.docx`: Explains methods for evaluating risks.
- `04-EISP-risk-mitigation-planning.docx`: Focuses on planning for risk mitigation.
- `05-EISP-risk-communication.docx`: Discusses strategies for effective risk communication.
- `06-EISP-risk-management-implementation.docx`: Outlines the implementation of risk management strategies.
